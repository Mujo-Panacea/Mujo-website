MuJo Panacea is building the leading platform for objective, data-driven musculoskeletal rehabilitation, starting with the shoulder. This page is the investor-facing summary.

## The opportunity

Musculoskeletal rehabilitation is a large, fragmented market with a durable structural problem: providers scale by adding therapists, and therapists cost money. Connected devices break that constraint. In the United States alone, physical therapy services is a **~$50bn market (2024), forecast to grow at roughly 5% annually**.

MuJo's core insight &mdash; protected by a patent family filed in 2012 and granted from 2017 across the US, UK/Europe, and China &mdash; is that the shoulder cannot be effectively rehabilitated in a single plane of motion. Every conventional strength-training machine in a clinical or gym setting does exactly that. MuJo trains the joint over its full envelope, and captures every rep as clinical-grade data.

## Where we are

- **Installed footprint** &mdash; approximately 12 devices already in the field across NHS trusts, orthopaedic hospitals, elite sport institutes, and private MSK clinics. These currently operate as mechanical units; redeploying the updated Mujo 1.5 Android software reactivates their connected screen and data-capture layer.
- **Additional fleet** &mdash; a further 20 devices in storage (3 built and ready to ship, 17 awaiting assembly), giving a ready pipeline for near-term deployment once fitted with the same Mujo 1.5 update.
- **Regulatory** &mdash; FDA listing under product code IKK (Class II, 510(k)-exempt) and UK MHRA registration (CA015151, Class I) in place.
- **Independent evidence** &mdash; a clinical feasibility study at the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital (2016), published as *Gilbert, Hauptmannova & Jaggi, BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 19:133 (2018)*; evaluation work at Coventry University (2015) and the University of Manchester; structured use at the English Institute of Sport (2017).
- **IP** &mdash; core mechanism protected by **US Patent 9,776,034** (*Exercise apparatus*, granted 2017), with corresponding protection in the UK/Europe (2683451) and China (ZL2012800227917). All software is developed and owned in-house by MuJo Panacea.
- **Mujo 1.5** &mdash; Android clinical release in structured pilot with NHS and sports partners, generating fresh evidence for the 2.0 submission.

## Where we are going

Two things unlock the next stage of the business:

1. **Mujo 2.0.** A motorised, sensor-enabled system on the SaMD pathway, in active development under a Knowledge Transfer Partnership with an academic partner. Targeting EU MDR Class IIa/IIb and the US FDA 510(k) route.
2. **Beyond the shoulder.** The same multi-axial approach applies to the hip, knee, and spine. Each represents a similarly large, similarly under-served rehabilitation category.

## The ask

**MuJo Panacea Limited** (company number **17165284**, registered in England & Wales) is raising a **£1,000,000 seed round through new equity, offering 20% of the company at a £4,000,000 post-money valuation**.

Use of funds:

1. **Redeploy the updated Mujo 1.5 Android software** &mdash; including a new Bluetooth serial connector &mdash; across the ~12 devices already in the field, reactivating their connected data-capture layer.
2. **Assemble and activate the 20-device stored fleet** (3 ready to ship, 17 requiring assembly) with the same Mujo 1.5 update.
3. **Deploy 10 of the newly activated devices to high-profile hospital and elite sports sites** across rugby union and rugby league, generating performance and outcomes data alongside the Mujo 2.0 programme.
4. **Progress the Mujo 2.0 motorisation programme** &mdash; through the Knowledge Transfer Partnership route where eligible, or direct hiring of software and mechanical engineers where it is not.
