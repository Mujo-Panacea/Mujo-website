**Status: in development under the Mujo 2.0 programme.**

Every MuJo device captures rich session-level movement data &mdash; rep-by-rep angle, accuracy, timing, and full movement traces. On the Mujo 1.5 platform, that data is already flowing to the cloud for clinician review. The next step is to turn it into a working tool for the clinician themselves.

## What we are building

A web dashboard designed for physiotherapists, MSK service leads, and rehabilitation clinicians:

- **A single view per patient** &mdash; every session, every exercise, every rep, plotted against the prescribed protocol
- **Compliance and progress tracking** &mdash; at a glance across a caseload, so exceptions surface without extra effort
- **Range-of-motion trajectories** &mdash; the trend that matters most, made visible without a spreadsheet

## And where the predictive model comes in

The dataset we are accumulating &mdash; consented, de-identified, and structured &mdash; supports the development of models that could help clinicians make better-informed decisions:

- Estimating time-to-goal for individual rehabilitation pathways
- Flagging plateaus early enough to adjust the prescription
- Benchmarking a patient's progression against comparable cohorts

We will only make specific claims about model performance once they have been established in clinical study. This work sits on the SaMD (Software as a Medical Device) pathway alongside the Mujo 2.0 device programme.

## Timing

Development runs alongside the Mujo 2.0 hardware programme under the KTP. Any commercial availability is subject to regulatory approval and clinical validation.
