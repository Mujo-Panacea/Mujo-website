MUJO Panacea is building the leading platform for objective, data-driven musculoskeletal rehabilitation. This page is the investor-facing summary.

## The company at a glance

*Editorial content to follow — the page will cover:*

- **What MUJO is:** a clinically grounded, hardware-plus-software system for shoulder rehabilitation
- **Where we are:** Mujo 1.5 (Android clinical release) in pilot; Mujo 2.0 (motorised, sensor-enabled) in development under a Knowledge Transfer Partnership programme
- **Regulatory posture:** ISO 13485 direction, EU MDR route mapped, existing FDA listing under product code IKK
- **Installed base:** approximately 12 devices across NHS, private physio, and sports institutions
- **Team:** Gerard Kool (CEO), Michael Sasserini (CFO), Andre Santos (CMO), and technical leads under the KTP academic partnership

## Roadmap

- **Mujo 1.5** — Android clinical release, re-enabling the installed fleet, structured pilot programme with NHS and sports partners
- **Mujo 2.0** — full motorised system with integrated sensors; SaMD-qualifying; targeting EU MDR Class IIa / FDA Class II route to market
- **Beyond 2.0** — lower limb device support, clinician-facing predictive analytics dashboard
