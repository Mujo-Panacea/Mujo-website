MuJo Panacea is building the leading platform for objective, data-driven musculoskeletal rehabilitation, starting with the shoulder. This page is the investor-facing summary.

## The opportunity

Musculoskeletal rehabilitation is a large, fragmented market with a durable structural problem: providers scale by adding therapists, and therapists cost money. Connected devices break that constraint. In the United States alone, physical therapy is a ~$30bn market growing at ~7% annually.

MuJo's core insight, patented since 2014, is that the shoulder cannot be effectively rehabilitated in a single plane of motion. Every conventional strength-training machine in a clinical or gym setting does exactly that. MuJo trains the joint over its full envelope &mdash; and captures every rep as clinical-grade data.

## Where we are

- **Installed footprint** &mdash; approximately 12 devices in field across NHS trusts, orthopaedic hospitals, elite sport institutes, and private MSK clinics
- **Regulatory** &mdash; FDA listing under product code IKK (Class II) and UK MHRA registration in place
- **Independent evidence** &mdash; positive results from clinical studies at the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital, evaluation work at Coventry University and the University of Manchester, and structured use at the English Institute of Sport
- **IP** &mdash; core mechanism protected by US Patent 8,821,357
- **Mujo 1.5** &mdash; Android clinical release in structured pilot with NHS and sports partners, generating fresh evidence for the 2.0 submission

## Where we are going

Two things unlock the next stage of the business:

1. **Mujo 2.0.** A motorised, sensor-enabled system on the SaMD pathway, in active development under a Knowledge Transfer Partnership with an academic partner. Targeting EU MDR Class IIa/IIb and the US FDA 510(k) route.
2. **Beyond the shoulder.** The same multi-axial approach applies to the hip, knee, and spine. Each represents a similarly large, similarly under-served rehabilitation category.
