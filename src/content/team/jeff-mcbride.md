Jeff brings three decades of experience in medical device regulatory affairs, quality, and operational leadership across the life sciences sector. With deep expertise in UK, EU, and US regulatory frameworks, he has delivered **UKCA**, **CE**, and **FDA** registrations and built quality systems compliant with **ISO 13485**, **EN 62304**, **ISO 27001**, and **ISO 14971** for clients spanning mobile, web, and games-console platforms.

His client work includes **Oncoassist**, **Medopad**, **Hinge Health**, **patientMpower**, **Atlas Biomed**, and **Peppy**, alongside ten years with MuJo.

As Chief Operating Officer at MuJo, Jeff draws on this regulatory and operational depth to guide the company's growth — mentoring the team, shaping strategy, and ensuring products meet global regulatory standards at every stage of development.
