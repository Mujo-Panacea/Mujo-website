Jeff McBride is an experienced medical-device regulatory and quality specialist with more than three decades of involvement in international regulatory affairs, compliance, and operational leadership across the life-sciences sector.

Jeff has developed Quality Systems to meet **ISO 13485**, **EN 62304**, **ISO 27001**, and **ISO 14971** requirements for medical-device development across mobile, web-based, and games-console apps ("for mHealth clients") — including CE Mark attainment, Data Protection registration, and security-standards verification. He provides mentoring, market analysis, strategising, and FDA registration support for SME medical-device developers.

Clients include **Oncoassist, Medopad, Hinge Health, patientMpower, AOS, Peppy** and many others, including ten years with MuJo to date.

As Chief Operating Officer at MuJo, Jeff brings this extensive regulatory and operational experience to support the company's growth — ensuring products are developed and delivered in compliance with global regulatory standards while enabling efficient scaling of the organisation's commercial and technical operations.
