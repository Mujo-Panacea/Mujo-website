## About the product

### What is MuJo?

MuJo is a shoulder rehabilitation system: a mechanical device with a patented multi-axial cam and a tablet application that guides the patient through their prescribed exercise. Every rep is measured, and every session is recorded to the clinician's dashboard.

### Who is it for?

Clinicians running MSK services (NHS or private), sports institutions and clubs with in-house medical teams, and rehabilitation research programmes. Patients interact with the device via the tablet, but MuJo is procured and prescribed by clinicians.

*If you're a clinician or a sports institution, [get in touch](/contact/) &mdash; we're happy to discuss a trial or demo.*

### Is MuJo a medical device?

Yes. It is FDA-listed as a Class II device under product code IKK in the United States and MHRA-registered in the United Kingdom. Software development follows ISO 13485 and IEC 62304.

### What does the device measure?

Range of motion (in shoulder and elbow), movement accuracy against the prescribed pathway, timing, rep counts, and full time-series movement traces per rep. All uploaded to the clinician-facing backend after each session.

## About the programme

### What is the difference between Mujo 1.5 and Mujo 2.0?

**Mujo 1.5** is the current-generation software running on modern Android tablets, connected wirelessly to the existing MuJo hardware. It re-enables the installed fleet and captures structured clinical evidence during the 2026 pilot programme.

**Mujo 2.0** is the next-generation *hardware and software* system: motorised, sensor-enabled, and qualifying as Software-as-a-Medical-Device. It is in development under a Knowledge Transfer Partnership with an academic partner and targets EU MDR and US FDA routes to market.

### Is there a clinician dashboard?

There will be. The dashboard and predictive analytics work is in development alongside the Mujo 2.0 hardware programme. Read more on the [Dashboard and Predictive Model page](/technology/dashboard-and-predictive-model/).

*If you would like to participate in the pilot cohort for the dashboard, [get in touch](/contact/).*
