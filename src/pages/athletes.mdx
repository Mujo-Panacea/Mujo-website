## Objective shoulder assessment

The MuJo system captures range of motion, movement accuracy, and rep-level performance across the full shoulder envelope. That means:

- **Baseline benchmarks** for individual athletes and squads &mdash; a repeatable snapshot of shoulder function
- **Post-injury rehabilitation tracking** &mdash; every session logged, every progression visible
- **Objective return-to-play criteria** &mdash; movement data supplements clinical judgement, it does not replace it

</MediaRow>

## Where MuJo is already in use

- **English Institute of Sport** &mdash; installed at Lilleshall National Sports Centre; used with Olympic-track athletes and Team GB squads
- **Wasps Rugby** &mdash; adopted for post-operative shoulder dislocation rehabilitation in a professional rugby environment
- **GB Weightlifting affiliate The Iron Club** &mdash; supporting overhead-load athletes with objective shoulder monitoring
