Artros Healthfocus Bewegingscentrum ‘Movement Centre’ provides corporate health training, physical assessments, functional capacity screenings, medical examinations and return to work services to employers and workers operating in the construction industry.

The centre is a leading provider of workman’s compensation services, training on average 4,000 patients each year, and is recognised and approved by the Construction Industry Training Fund. Artros presented MUJO at the 2015 construction fair ‘BouwBeurs 2015’.
