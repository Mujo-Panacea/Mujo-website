Development of MUJO’s connected devices completes in October following 6 months of work on the Innovate UK collaborative R&D project which began in April. The devices now connect to secure electronic medical records and are registered as Class I Medical Devices with the MHRA, ahead of the [clinical feasibility study at The Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital](https://www.mujofitness.com/evidence/2016/11/30/positive-results-reported-clinical-study/), Stanmore in 2016.
