MuJo Panacea Ltd is the UK-based medical-device business behind the MuJo shoulder rehabilitation system. Our purpose is straightforward: to help people maximise their movement for a better quality of life.

<MediaRow image="/images/site/mujo-device-5.jpg" alt="The MuJo shoulder rehabilitation device">

## What we do

We build hardware and software for objective, connected musculoskeletal rehabilitation. Our devices are installed in NHS trusts, orthopaedic hospitals, elite sports institutes, and private MSK clinics. Our software turns movement into data that clinicians can act on.

</MediaRow>

## How we got here

The multi-axial cam at the heart of the MuJo device was invented in 2006 by mechanical engineer Douglas Higgins to solve a problem no conventional exercise machine could: how to load and train a complex joint through its full natural range. MuJo was founded in 2011 to apply the mechanism to the shoulder and, over time, to other joints.

Since then the device has been installed across the UK and internationally, registered with the UK MHRA and the US FDA, protected by patents including US 8,821,357, and studied independently at the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital, the University of Manchester, and Coventry University.

## Where we are today

MuJo Panacea Ltd now leads the next stage of the company. Two programmes run in parallel:

- **Mujo 1.5** &mdash; a modern Android release that re-enables the installed fleet on Bluetooth and drives a structured clinical pilot with NHS and sports partners
- **Mujo 2.0** &mdash; a motorised, sensor-enabled successor system, in active development under a Knowledge Transfer Partnership with an academic partner, targeting EU MDR and FDA routes to market

Beyond 2.0, the roadmap extends the multi-axial approach to the hip, knee, and spine.

## The five pillars

Everything MuJo does sits on five principles:

- **Health** &mdash; the outcome we care about most
- **Science** &mdash; independently studied, not just marketed
- **Technology** &mdash; hardware and software built with clinical intent
- **Data** &mdash; connected, structured, and useful to clinicians
- **People** &mdash; patients, athletes, and the clinicians who work with them
