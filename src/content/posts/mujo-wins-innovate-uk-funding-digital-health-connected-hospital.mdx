In October 2014, MUJO won £436,000 of funding after successfully entering the Innovate UK ‘Digital Health in a Connected Hospital’ call, which offered investment in collaborative R&D projects which stimulate innovation in the UK digital health sector.

Starting on 1st April 2015, the project is being led by MUJO in collaboration with the Royal National Orthopaedic Hospital NHS Trust as Clinical Partner, The University of Manchester as Academic Partner and Qinec Ltd as Commercial Partner.

The project is being co-funded by an award of £436,000 from Innovate UK, the UK’s innovation agency.
