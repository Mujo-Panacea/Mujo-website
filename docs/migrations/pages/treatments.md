_Title: Shoulder Treatments_

We treat a variety of shoulder, upper back and neck conditions. All of our shoulder treatments are individualised to you and your health needs.  
We assess shoulder health with our three point check. We assess strength, range of motion and motor control accuracy. A report is provided with data to represent your shoulder health.  
Just as we endeavour to keep our heart and bodies healthy, we need to treat our shoulders in the same manner and keep them healthy. Our wellbeing program does just that. Each program is tailored to you.

## The main shoulder treatments

---

The MUJO Frozen Shoulder Treatment plan is designed to provide faster pain relief, restore range of motion, strength and function in comparison to conventional physiotherapy. The plan is tailored to your individual needs so that you receive maximum benefit and experience improvements from the very first session.  
The MUJO Shoulder Impingement Treatment plan is designed to be the best course of treatment available. Current research shows surgery is not more affective than physiotherapy. Our individualised treatments with the use of our patented smart technology ensures the MUJO treatment is the best.  
The MUJO upper back and neck pain is designed to target the root cause of your pain. In most cases, poor posture while working at a desk or poor use of backpacks leads to muscle fatigue, weakness and a lack of good motor control. The MUJO treatment aims to restore excellent motor control to counteract the changes that cause you pain and discomfort.  
The MUJO shoulder instability treatment plan is designed to engage the stabilising muscles whilst performing controlled motions. The individualised treatment plan encourages motor learning to ensure an increased functional stability of the glenohumeral joint.  

The MUJO technology can treat majority of shoulder, upper back and neck conditions. These include but are not limited to rotator cuff weakness and muscle atrophy. Get in touch with us to discuss how we can tailor a program suited to your needs.
