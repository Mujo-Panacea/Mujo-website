The MUJO shoulder devices were installed at the English Institute of Sport Lilleshall National Sports Centre as part of the Innovate UK project to assess their use in a rehabilitation setting. Elite athletes training for the Rio 2016 Olympics including GB Gymnastics and Archery GB train at the facility, which is also co-located with The Royal British Legion ‘Battle Back’ Centre.
